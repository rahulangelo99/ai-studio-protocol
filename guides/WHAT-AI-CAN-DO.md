# What your AI can do: a menu

You don't need to remember any of this. Say what you want in your own words and the AI picks the tool.
This list shows how much is possible, so you aim bigger.

**Tag key:** 🟢 = works on any Claude account · 🔵 = works in Claude Code / the desktop app (it can use the computer) · 🟣 = only on Rahul's setup (ask Rahul to run it)

---

## 1. Think with you
| Ask for… | What you get |
|---|---|
| "Here's my messy idea, help me shape it" 🟢 | A clean brief: problem, who it's for, why now, first step |
| "Poke holes in this" 🟢 | The weak spots, the risks, and how to fix each |
| "Give me 10 wild versions of this" 🟢 | Variations from safe to extreme |
| "Research this properly" 🟢 | Deep web research with sources (market, competitors, prices, laws) |
| "Turn this into a plan" 🟢 | Steps, costs, timeline, what to do first |

## 2. Make things you can show people

| A short promo or explainer video 🔵 | Animated video with text, music and voiceover |
| AI images and video 🟣 | Generated on Rahul's own machine (FLUX, SDXL, LTX video) |
| Diagrams 🟢 | Flowcharts, "how it works" pictures, mind maps |
| Documents 🟢 | Word, Excel, PDF, proposals, contracts, business plans |

## Three rules that make you fast
1. **Write like you talk.** Typing first; Rahul will switch you to voice (Windows: Win+H · Mac: tap 🌐 twice) when you're ready. Messy is fine, the AI sharpens it.
2. **Say the dream first, the limits second.** "I want X. Budget is Y. Deadline is Z."
3. **Ask for a first version now.** You react to something real faster than you can describe it perfectly.

## Two safety rules
- Never paste passwords, bank details, Aadhaar/PAN, or private keys into the AI.
- The AI asks before it sends, buys, posts or deletes anything. That's on purpose; read before you say yes.
