# Getting started (5 minutes, once)

You'll get your own **AI-Studio** folder. Everything you make is saved there, organized by idea, with a full history. Your AI follows the studio rules on its own every time, so you never repeat setup.

## Step 1: Unzip
Double-click `SG-kit.zip` in Downloads. You get a folder `SG-kit`.

## Step 2: Build your studio
Open **Claude Code** (any folder is fine for this one step) and type, with **S** or **G** as your letter:

```text
Please run: zsh ~/Downloads/SG-kit/setup-studio.sh S
Then tell me what it printed, in plain words.
```

A Finder window opens on your new **AI-Studio** folder. It also prints your **phone channel name**; note it for the ntfy app (optional). If it says "Version history: OFF", click Install on the pop-up, then run the step again.

## Step 3: Work from your studio, always
Start a **new Claude Code session**, choose the folder **AI-Studio** (in your home folder), and keep the permission setting on **ask before acting**.
First message:

```text
Hi, I'm S. Let's start.
```

Your AI will:
- say **"Message line ready."** (connected to Rahul)
- ask for up to 3 problems or ideas you've had
- start each reply with **"Sharpened:"**, your message turned into a clear request. Watch how it changes; that's how you learn to ask well.
- mark what it's sure of: ✅ checked · 🟡 likely · ❓ unknown
- ask before **every** action outside your folder. A yes counts once. Saying no is always fine.

## Words that do things
| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| `log` | All your ideas and the latest progress |
| `where is it?` | Exact location of what you just made, opens in Finder |
| `wrap` | Closes the session properly: notes saved, next step written |
| `undo` | Shows the last change and offers to bring back the previous version |
| `stop` | Everything stops immediately |
| `send Rahul …` | Sends Rahul your message |
| `go big` | Allows big research or builds (uses more of your limit) |
| `make it bolder` / `10 wild versions` | Pushes the idea further |

## Typing now, voice later
Start by typing. Rahul will tell you when to switch to voice.
**To switch:** System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation → On. Then tap 🌐 (or Fn) twice in any text box and speak.

## Stay safe
- Never type passwords, OTPs, bank or card numbers, Aadhaar/PAN, or keys into the AI.
- Read before you say yes. If anything feels off, type `stop`.
- Lock your screen when you step away (Ctrl+Cmd+Q).
